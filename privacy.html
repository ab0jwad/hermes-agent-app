Privacy Policy – Hermes Agent (personal)

Last updated: November 1, 2026

  • This app is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.
  • Google user data it accesses (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets, Docs) is used only to carry out the owner's own requests.
  • To fulfil those requests, relevant content may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has configured.
  • Data is never sold, never shared with other third parties, and never used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
