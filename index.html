Hermes Agent (personal)

A private, personal-use OAuth client. It lets the owner's own Hermes Agent, running on the owner's computer, access the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Contacts, Sheets, Docs) at the owner's request.

It is not offered to anyone else.
